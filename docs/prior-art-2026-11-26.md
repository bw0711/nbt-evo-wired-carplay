# Wired CarPlay: who has already done what (search of 2026-11-26)

We searched GitHub (code search: `vdev-medialaunch`, `umass-enum`, `CARPLAY_USB_ON`, `iap2ncm-omap543x`,
`nbt_carplay`, `com.apple.carkit.service`), the web and forums.

**Result:** there is no open wired CarPlay for NBT **Evo**. **A closed commercial one exists:** a commercial remote-coding service showed wired
CarPlay on the Evo with a real iPhone on 9 October 2026 (item 3a). So this is certainly possible on this hardware. But there is a working open receiver
of wired CarPlay for the **old NBT** (x86, QNX 6.5) — `nbt_carplay`. It works without an adapter, through the native
MFi chip. It can be ported to the Evo. The morning conclusion "our own implementation is unrealistic, there is no open receiver" was
**wrong**.

## 1. `nbt_carplay` — wired CarPlay on the old NBT ⭐

- Code: [JayJay3071/nbt-android-auto](https://github.com/JayJay3071/nbt-android-auto), folder `src/carplay/`, description
  [docs/CARPLAY.md](https://github.com/JayJay3071/nbt-android-auto/blob/main/docs/CARPLAY.md). The author of the code is
  Valentin Radu, the source is in the fork [valinet/nbt-android-auto](https://github.com/valinet/nbt-android-auto).
  The license of this part is **GPL-3.0-only**.
- Working since 2026-11-23: F30 + iPhone 15 Pro Max on iOS 17.7.2. Since 24.11 it also works for a second author: F07 2014 +
  iPhone 17 Air on iOS 27.0.1. There is picture, sound, iDrive controller, Siri, the car's microphone, calls, track titles with
  cover art and arrows in the instrument cluster.
- **How it is done — without role switch, the head unit stays the USB host:**
  1. Vendor request `0x52` (IN, value 0, index 4). The iPhone reconnects with extra configurations: a USB multiplexer
     (`ff/fe/02`) and CDC NCM (`02/0d`).
  2. Through the multiplexer: lockdown, pairing ("Trust?"), TLS, the service `com.apple.carkit.service`.
  3. On top of it iAP2: identification as a CarPlay car, MFi chip signature, `CarPlayStartSession`.
  4. The network over NCM through lwIP inside the process (IPv6 link-local), the car's network is not touched. Then AirPlay on port 7000:
     pair-setup (SRP), pair-verify, auth-setup by the chip, H.264 screen, LPCM sound, microphone, iDrive controller.
- The MFi chip there is 2.0B. The certificate expired in 2021, but the iPhone accepts it. A signature takes about 1.5 s.
- The load on the old NBT is about 38% CPU, 30 frames/s (at 60 the decoder could not keep up). iOS 27 required setting the format
  NCM16 (`SET_NTB_FORMAT`).
- There is a step-by-step check tool: `nbt_carplay_probe acp | usb | usb carplay | lockdown | carkit`. The tests
  `test_ncm_host.c` and `test_airplay_host.c` run on a PC.
- Basis: [shilapi/xcertplay](https://github.com/shilapi/xcertplay) (GPL-3.0, CarPlay on an Android head unit with an MFi chip) and
  [LIVI](https://f-io.github.io/LIVI/).

### What of this matches on our Evo (📄 checked against the firmware)

| Part of `nbt_carplay` | On the old NBT | On NBT Evo |
|---|---|---|
| MFi chip | `/dev/i2c/i2c_0/acp`, 0x20 | `/dev/i2c/i2c_1/acp`, 0x20 (`carplay.ini`). ❗ It is already held by the factory wireless `mm-ipod` (`har-i2c-iap2`) |
| Sound and microphone | `liba2i_client.so` (`A2I_Client_*`) | The same API: `/opt/mm/lib/liba2i_client.so`, the factory CarPlay runs on it too (`libconn_carplay_audio_a2i.so`) |
| USB | `libusbdi`, host | `libusbdi.so.2`, xHCI host. `vdev-medialaunch` also grabs the iPhone as an iPod, and `nbt_carplay` knows how to get around this |
| Video | `nbt_decoder` (the NBT's own decoder) | ❗ Replace: mm-renderer / IVA-HD on the 1280 window, like the factory CarPlay and Android Auto for the Evo (see item 2) |
| iDrive controller | `nbt_knob` | Our input from full-screen apps |
| Build | QNX 6.5 x86 | QNX 6.5 ARMv7. We have the toolchain |

## 2. Android Auto on NBT Evo (Supra / iDrive 6)

[Sq00ky/Supra-iDrive6-Android-Auto](https://github.com/Sq00ky/Supra-iDrive6-Android-Auto) — native Android Auto
on NBT Evo. License "other". It works through the Wi‑Fi of the factory CarPlay, there is no wired CarPlay in it. Useful as a
sample of the Evo parts: video through mm-renderer on the 1280 layer, sound through A2I, microphone. It writes about
`vdev-medialaunch`: on connection it asks the device once for AOA and creates `/dev/android*`.

  instructions of binary patches and proxy libraries for the iDrive controller, layers and touch. The same dead end as ours in October.

  SRP6a) to drive Android Auto through the head unit's wireless CarPlay. This is the opposite direction, not a cable.
- **A member of a commercial coding service, 15.10.2026** (his signature advertises BMW NBT / EVO tools, SSH, FSC, remote access, CarPlay and Android
  Auto). He wrote that a cheap Chinese USB adapter (in the armrest) turns Android Auto into CarPlay, together with head unit patches, with
  direct sound in PCM format without double conversion. Asked whether he made wireless CarPlay wired over USB, he answered
  that he had just implemented wired CP support and that wireless is available as before.
  - The post has 4 photos and a 28-byte file `wired_cp.txt` attached (probably a link). I did not download the file.
- **A video by the same commercial remote-coding service, 09.10.2026** — about Android Auto via an adapter and wired CarPlay on the Evo.
  Its description says that wired (USB) Apple CarPlay on BMW NBT EVO was installed remotely. The chapters show: an iPhone on USB, nothing happens;
  installing wired CarPlay; the iPhone connects over USB. So it was a **real iPhone**, not only through an adapter. The same
  service also has a video about native wired Android Auto on the Evo. (Links not kept here.)

  - 🤔 The phrase about direct PCM sound without double conversion looks more like a custom receiver that asks the

  native wired and wireless Android Auto on EVO ID5/6 and wireless AA on the old NBT. No sources

## 4. Other Harman/QNX cars

- [joeyQuery/MHI2-Wireless-CarPlay](https://github.com/joeyQuery/MHI2-Wireless-CarPlay) (Audi MHI2, Harman). There the factory
  wired CarPlay goes through `devu-iap2ncm-tegra3-ci.so`, the interface `carplay0` and
  `MDNS_DIRECTLINK_IFACE`. This is the same scheme we found in the Evo (`ncm0` + `mdnsd` + `CARPLAY_DIRECT_IFNAME`), except
  that there Harman enabled it.
- Reference QNX CAR configs for role switching (`usblauncher/otg.lua` with `iap2ncm`) exist in public repositories
  (links not kept here, since those repositories appear to contain proprietary QNX SDK and vendor files).

## What this means for us

Now there are two paths:

**A. Enable the factory stack** (our analysis of `medialaunch` and Connectivity).
- Pros: BMW's native CarPlay, native sound, Siri, HUD/instrument cluster.
- Cons: everything rests on the undocumented state machine of `medialaunch` (race, `mm-ipod`, `mdnsd`), and it can only
  be checked on the car.

**B. Port `nbt_carplay` to the Evo.**
- Pros: the protocol is already solved and proven on two cars with iOS 17 and 27. No role switch, no port flip, no
  firmware patches. Sound through the same A2I, the MFi chip is the same. The protocol part can be run in tests on the Mac.
- Cons:
  - the video (mm-renderer/IVA-HD) and input must be replaced;
  - the MFi chip is shared with the factory wireless CarPlay — ❓ whether they can be combined;
  - the CPU load on the Evo is unknown;
  - this is our own window over iDrive, not BMW's native CarPlay;
  - **GPL-3.0**: for yourself — no restrictions, but this code cannot be included in paid closed apps. It may be distributed
    only under the GPL with sources.

**Done on the evening of 26.11:** `nbt_carplay_probe` was ported to the Evo — `wired-carplay/probe-evo/` (GPL-3.0, separate from
the paid apps).
- The MFi chip is read as in the factory driver: Harman `devctl 0xC80C0502`, node `/dev/i2c/i2c_1/acp`.
- The probe takes the common chip lock `/dev/shm_acp_lock`, the same one as the factory processes and `acpd`.
- The ARMv7 build and the import check passed.
- In IDEmu the probe starts, correctly refuses without the chip and USB, the lock was checked with a test replacement of `acpd`.
- The next step is the car, the order and risks are in `wired-carplay/probe-evo/README.md`.

🤔 My recommendation: path B now looks more reliable. A cheap first step is to build `nbt_carplay_probe` for ARM and run `acp` and `usb carplay`
on the car. This test will show whether the Evo's chip answers and whether the iPhone gives NCM through `0x52` on the
Evo's port. It is almost read-only. There is one risk: `probe acp` makes a test signature on the chip used by the
factory wireless `mm-ipod`, so run it with wireless CarPlay turned off. Path A is worth keeping as the
fallback.
