# Car visit 27.11.2026: factory CarPlay over the cable with an entry in iDrive

Logs: `car/runs/20261127-105910/` (summary.txt, bridge.txt, slog-live-bridge.txt, pf-check.txt, files-nbt-aa/ with the
car's HMI). The bridge is the same as on 26.11 (`nbt_carplay` SHA 30a98e1e…), without Connectivity patches.

## Main result
**The "Apple CarPlay" entry in the iDrive main menu is there, and the return after "minimise" works, without a single
patch.** The factory receiver accepted the session over the cable as its own. These work: the picture, the menu entry
and the return (the HMI sends `Sending Request UI command`), CarPlay as a source in "Media/Radio", the iDrive controller
(switches tracks), Siri from the steering-wheel button (`Performing Siri Action: ButtonDown/ButtonUp`).
**Not working: audio and microphone stay in the phone** (music and Siri play and listen on the iPhone).

## The condition under which it works (checked)
1. In iDrive "Mobile devices → <iPhone>" the mode **"Apple CarPlay"** is selected (not "BMW iDrive").
2. The iPhone is connected to the car over Bluetooth. Then the head unit is "looking for the phone": once a second the
   plugin writes `Device with BT Address=… is not found in available device list`.
3. The session over the cable (`CarPlayStartSession` from the bridge) arrives **inside this window**.

Then the log shows `CarPlay Session created` → `UP and RUNNING` and **no** `Disconnection FAILED` (the head unit does
not try to close the session) — exactly as predicted by the analysis in `../docs/research/factory-like-device-list-re-2026-11-26.md`
(DipoState ACTIVATION → ACTIVE).

## What happened, step by step
- **Step 0 (read).** The USB/CarPlay files on the head unit are the same as on 26.11. `conn-check`: the running
  Connectivity was found (`opt/conn/bin/Connectivity`), c1/c2 in factory state, the byte-for-byte comparison of
  functions passed. `pf-check`: `filter_OBD` on the car is **not empty** — it holds the rules that let our SSH through
  over ENET (`dwc0`, port 22), so HIDE_WIFI refused to touch it (as designed). `nbt-aa-pull`: took the car's HmiMain3D
  (SHA da4b1321…), libnbt-aa-cp.so, the logs.
- **Step 1, first run: no entry.** The head unit looked for the phone until 10:59:29 and stopped: with Wi-Fi off,
  wireless CarPlay did not connect, and the head unit itself switched the phone to the "BMW iDrive" mode (visible in
  "Mobile devices"). The session arrived outside the window → `Disconnection FAILED as there is no currently selected
  device` → no entry (as on 26.11).
- **Step 1, repeat: the entry is there.** The owner selected "Apple CarPlay" for the iPhone; the script waited for the
  search (`WAIT_HU`, 15 s) and started the bridge; the session start went out after ~26 s, inside the window. On the
  iPhone "Turn On Wi-Fi to Use Wireless CarPlay" pops up (dismiss it).
- **Run 3:** after the session ended the head unit started looking for the phone again by itself (the mode stayed "Apple
  CarPlay"), and the repeat went the same way. Siri from the wheel and the controller work; audio does not.
- Steps 2 (bridge-ctrl), 3 (c1) and 4 (Wi-Fi on) were not needed / postponed.

## Instrument cluster, head-up display, steering-wheel buttons: what is visible
- The iDrive controller works in CarPlay and switches tracks; the Siri button on the wheel works (both go through the
  AirPlay session itself).
- **The track buttons on the wheel do not work, and there is no track title on the head-up display or in the
  instrument cluster** (the media widget with arrows is there, empty).
- The cause, from the 26.11 wireless CarPlay log: there Connectivity holds a **second channel with the iPhone — iAP2 over
  Wi-Fi** (`iap2` with `config=/var/opt/conn/carplay/iap2_Wifi.cfg, ppsdir=/pps/services/multimedia/iap2_wifi`,
  authentication by the factory MFi chip). "What is playing", the track commands and probably the navigation arrows go
  through it. Over the cable the iPhone runs iAP2 with our bridge, and the factory Connectivity does not get it.
- **Next:** the bridge does not run iAP2 itself but hands it to the factory Connectivity as iAP2 over the network (as
  over Wi-Fi); then the head unit introduces itself to the iPhone. Perhaps this will fix audio too. The config
  `iap2_Wifi.cfg` from the car is in `runs/20261126-195127/files-conn/`.

## Audio and microphone: what is visible
- Wireless CarPlay (26.11): after `Setup` come `[CarPlay][AudioStream] AudioStreamPrepare … streamType = 101` and
  `[CarPlay][AudioConverter]` (AAC). Over the cable there is not a single `AudioStream`/`AudioConverter` line: the
  iPhone did not open any audio stream to the head unit, although the modes `mainAudio controller` and `speech
  controller (recognizing/speaking)` are there.
- The original bridge project (its own receiver) gets streams 100/101 (LPCM) from the iPhone over the cable with the same
  accessory description, so the problem is not our identification but the factory receiver.
- The difference: `Session is UP and RUNNING on Transport: ?` over the cable against `Transport: WiFi`. The main
  hypothesis: the receiver does not know the channel type of tap0 and does not offer the iPhone audio formats (or offers
  only the wireless ones). By the 26.11 analysis the channel type is set by `SocketGetInterfaceInfo`
  (`CARPLAY_DIRECT_IFNAME`). **Next: analyse libconn_carplay — where the channel type affects /info and the audio
  formats — and an in-memory patch following the conn_patch scheme.**

## After cleanup: wireless for comparison and a probe of the factory iAP2 driver
- **Wireless CarPlay (Wi-Fi on):** audio in the speakers, the track buttons on the wheel work. The rule counters of uap0
  (`pf-counters-*.txt`): TCP 7000 (control), 6030 (screen, 7.6 MB), 5001, UDP 6000 (audio, 0.95 MB), 6020. Over the
  cable the iPhone also went to 7000, 6030 and 5001, but did not open audio streams.
- **How the head unit runs iAP2 in a wireless session:** right after `AirPlay session started … T=WiFi` OSCAR starts
  the package `CONN_CARPLAY_WLAN_DRIVER` = `/opt/conn/bin/carplay/mm-ipod -a har-i2c-iap2 -m /dev/ipod_wifi -d iap2,config=
  /var/opt/conn/carplay/iap2_Wifi.cfg,ppsdir=/pps/services/multimedia/iap2_wifi,probe -t bt,path=/dev/carplayiap,type=wifi`
  (+ LD_LIBRARY_PATH as for Connectivity). iAP2 goes inside the CarPlay session through `/dev/carplayiap`; the state is
  in the PPS `/pps/services/multimedia/iap2_wifi/{device,nowplaying,communications,location,vehicle}` (captured,
  `pps-iap2-wifi.txt`).
- **Probe (with the owner's "yes"):** in the cable session this `mm-ipod` was started by hand with the same arguments.
  It opened `/dev/carplayiap` and sent `iAP2Probe`, there was no answer → `Probe query failed`. **Over the cable the
  iPhone does not run iAP2 inside the CarPlay session** (its iAP2 goes over USB to the bridge). So the instrument
  cluster / head-up display / steering-wheel buttons will work only if the bridge itself gives the factory the iAP2 data
  (for example "what is playing" in the PPS iap2_wifi and the track commands back) or hands it the whole iAP2. The
  driver was stopped by process number.
- **nbt-aa in the chain:** in `/var/opt/conn/carplay/carplay.ini` the section `[iAP2]` points not to the factory
  `libconn_iap2_wifi_qnx.so` but to `/var/opt/conn/carplay/libnbt-cpadapter.so` (the original is
  `carplay.ini.nbt-orig`). Both files and the library were taken to `files-27/`. The owner can roll back the nbt-aa
  changes; not needed for now.
- **Audio — the main lead:** the section `[Audio]` in `carplay.ini` offers the iPhone only AAC-ELD and AAC-LC (the set
  for wireless). The original bridge project gets uncompressed PCM from the iPhone over the cable. Hypothesis: over the
  cable the iPhone wants PCM and, not finding it, keeps the audio for itself. To check at home against the receiver code
  (which formats it understands); the fix is most likely PCM lines in `carplay.ini` and a restart of Connectivity (a
  write to flash, only with the owner's consent).
- Cleanup at the end: there are no files of ours, processes, rules or tap0 on the head unit.
- **Formats in the receiver (libconn_carplay.so, strings):** there is the full Apple PCM set (`PCM/16000/16/1` …
  `PCM/48000/24/…`), ALAC, AACLC, AACELD, OPUS and the log `Audio SETUP received, Audio Channel: %s; Audio Type: %s;
  Audio Format: %s; Input: %s` (it did not appear once over the cable). The table of words for `Format:` in the ini
  parser: ALAC, AACLC, AACELD, OPUS — how to set PCM (apparently through SR/Bit/Channel without these words), to check
  against the code before the edit. The owner's goal: uncompressed PCM over the cable, as in the factory wired CarPlay.
  Also the strings `iAP2 transport on CarPlay is supported`, `iAP2WiFiAdapterGet/Dispose` (the interface of the library
  from `[iAP2] LibPath`).
